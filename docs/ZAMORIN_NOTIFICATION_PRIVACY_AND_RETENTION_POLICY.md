# ZAMORIN CAFE ERP — NOTIFICATION PRIVACY & RETENTION POLICY
**Document ID**: `ZAM-POL-PRIVACY-RETENTION-012`  
**Version**: `1.0.0`  
**Status**: `AUTHORITATIVE`

---

## 1. Absolute Privacy Boundaries
1. **Personal Ledger Privacy**: Personal Ledger records, mutations, balances, and statements are MASTER-ONLY and are NEVER routed or included in automated emails.
2. **Payroll Privacy**: Bulk salary databases and bank account credentials are never transmitted in cleartext via email. Operations receives aggregate count/error status; staff receive notifications directing them to secure ERP login.
3. **Attendance Privacy**: Operational alerts include employee ID and cafe context rather than unnecessary PII.

## 2. Retention Schedules
- **SECURITY & INCIDENTS**: Retained for 7 years for compliance and audit integrity.
- **PROCUREMENT & VENDOR**: Retained for 5 years for tax and commercial reconciliation.
- **OPERATIONAL DIGESTS**: Retained for 90 days.
- **SYSTEM HEALTH HEARTBEATS**: Retained for 30 days.
